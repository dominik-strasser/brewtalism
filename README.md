# Brewtalism

**A Brutalist concrete enclosure for the Gaggia Classic EVO.**

Brewtalism is an experimental redesign of the Gaggia Classic EVO espresso machine, replacing its original metal housing with a custom concrete body.

Inspired by Brutalist architecture, raw concrete, and blocky, geometric forms, the goal is to create a minimalist, monolithic espresso machine with a bold, industrial aesthetic.

## The Concept

The idea is simple: take a classic espresso machine and give it a completely new body made from concrete.

The project combines 3D modeling, additive manufacturing, and concrete casting. Instead of directly manufacturing the final enclosure, the design is developed through 3D-printed prototypes before producing custom molds for casting the concrete components.

The design prioritizes simple geometric shapes, sharp edges, and exposed concrete surfaces.

## Design & Manufacturing Process

The project follows three main stages:

### 1. 3D Modeling

Design the enclosure using **Blender** and **Plasticity**.

The models will account for the internal components of the Gaggia Classic EVO, including mounting points, controls, ventilation, and access for maintenance.

### 2. Prototyping

3D print the enclosure components to validate:

- Dimensions and fitment
- Internal component clearance
- Mounting and assembly
- Overall proportions and appearance

The prototypes will help identify design issues before committing to concrete casting.

### 3. Concrete Casting

Once the design is validated, create 3D-printable molds for casting the final enclosure.

This stage includes mold design, casting, demolding, and surface finishing.

## Project Goals

- Create a distinctive, Brutalist-inspired concrete enclosure.
- Explore simple, blocky, and geometric forms.
- Preserve the functionality of the original Gaggia Classic EVO.
- Develop a reliable workflow from digital models to physical concrete parts.
- Design 3D-printable molds suitable for concrete casting.
- Document the process and share the models for others to explore.

## Safety & Disclaimer

This project involves modifying an electrical appliance that operates with mains voltage, high temperatures, and pressurized water.

Concrete has different electrical, thermal, and structural properties from the original metal enclosure. Adequate electrical safety, grounding of exposed conductive parts, insulation, ventilation, structural support, and maintenance access must be verified before operating the modified machine.

Brewtalism is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Gaggia.
